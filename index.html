Our Story

Born from a love of
simplicity & craft

01 Our roasting process

KŌHĪ was born in a small corner of Portland where we believed coffee could be more than a morning ritual—it could be a moment of mindful beauty.

We source single-origin beans from small farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Japan. Each batch is roasted in-house with obsessive attention to flavor profiles that honor the bean's natural character.

Our space is designed as a sanctuary—clean lines, warm wood, natural light, and the gentle rhythm of coffee being crafted by hand.
